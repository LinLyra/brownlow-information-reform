# Methods appendix

## Analysis universe

The panel contains one observation per player-match for 2022–2026. Historical expected votes are generated by the frozen champion model under rolling temporal validation; 2026 expected votes come from a forecast frozen before the Brownlow count. Existing forecasting artifacts are never retrained for this paper.

## Information indices

Each statistic is ranked within match using average percentile ranks and centred at 0.5. The primary visible index is the equal-weight mean of 16 mapped AFL-approved categories. The placebo index is the equal-weight mean of eight prespecified performance statistics absent from the approved list. Each composite is standardized over the full panel. No weight is selected using 2026 Brownlow outcomes.

## Structural-change estimator

The player-match regression is:

`votes ~ visible + placebo + visible×target + placebo×target + team_won + position FE + season FE`.

For the actual reform, `target=1` in 2026. Heteroskedasticity-consistent finite-sample corrected covariance is clustered by match. The visible-minus-placebo contrast uses the complete covariance matrix of the two interaction estimates.

## Pseudo reforms

For target years 2023–2026, the analysis uses an expanding sample beginning in 2022 and ending in the nominated target year. The target indicator equals one only in that final year. Feature construction, scaling, controls, clustering and estimator are unchanged. Three placebo dates are insufficient for a formal randomization-inference p-value; their role is descriptive benchmarking of ordinary temporal variation.

## Composite robustness

- R1 averages within-match z-scored raw features.
- R2 uses PCA first-component loadings learned only from 2022–2025 and applied unchanged to 2026.
- R3 uses fixed ridge weights learned from 2022–2025 Brownlow outcomes only and applied unchanged to 2026.
- Leave-one-stat-out removes each visible feature once without selecting a preferred subset.

## Coach–umpire alignment

Annual outcomes include player-match Spearman correlation, average within-match rank correlation, top-three overlap, three-vote winner agreement and extreme-disagreement match rate. The 2026 change is measured against the equal-weight mean of 2022–2025 annual estimates. Confidence intervals use 2,000 seeded match-cluster bootstrap replicates. Coach votes are treated as an independent assessment, not objective ground truth.

## Multiplicity and interpretation

The primary visible and placebo interaction p-values use Holm correction. Feature-level families are corrected separately where reported. Pseudo-year and robustness analyses are specification diagnostics rather than additional confirmatory hypotheses. Structural changes are not interpreted causally because there is one post-reform season and no untreated league control.
